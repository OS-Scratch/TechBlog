# Windows 11: hidden customization options
Windows 11 is one of the best operating systems. Period. Well, at least, that's my opinion. Some other users might not agree. But I just prefer its feature set. I've never seen anything like it. In this tutorial, we'll dive deeper in what customization options it offers.
## Customize your taskbar
Go to Start > Settings and navigate to Personalization. You are greeted with various options; select "Colours". Now, to do this, we must first set the standard Windows mode to dark, if you haven't done so. Go to Select mode and choose Custom. Choose Dark for Standard Windows Mode. Scroll down and select an accent color. Turn the switch on for "Show accent color in Start menu and taskbar" and watch your OS become filled with color!
## Lock screen: information at a glance
Ever wished you could see the weather report on your lock screen? Well, that is possible? Navigate to Lock screen and under Lock screeen status, choose an app. Press Win + L and get your information at a glance.
## Start: How to show less recommendations
Go to Start and select your layout. This is a pretty simple one, but really helpful.
